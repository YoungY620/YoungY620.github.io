## Jacquard

[Jacquard](https://github.com/keijiro/Jacquard) is an experimental Unity music tool by Keijiro. It was used as a creative reference for the town's audio and interactive presentation.

Creator contact: [Keijiro on Twitter](https://twitter.com/_kzr)

The site uses only this asset pack for shipped pixel artwork, UI, sound effects and pack music. Selected source sprites are normalized, cropped or composed into maps inside `public/assets/ninja-v1/` and `src/world/tiled/`.
